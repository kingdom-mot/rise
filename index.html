Welcome to Rise

A space to explore, understand, and reconnect with yourself.

Rise Somatic Therapy was created from a belief that meaningful therapy honors the whole person — mind, body, emotions, relationships, and lived experiences.

My approach is compassionate, collaborative, and trauma-informed. I believe you are the expert on your own experience, and therapy should provide space to explore, understand, and reconnect with yourself without judgment.

How I Can Help

Services & Approach

Individual Therapy

Somatic & Embodied Therapy

EMDR
