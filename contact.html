If You Need Support Right Now

Crisis & emergency resources

Rise Somatic Therapy is not an emergency or crisis service, and messages sent by email or through the client portal are not monitored around the clock. If you are in crisis or experiencing a medical or mental health emergency:

  • Call or text 988 to reach the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, available 24/7
  • Call 911 or go to your nearest emergency room
  • Text HOME to 741741 to reach the Crisis Text Line
