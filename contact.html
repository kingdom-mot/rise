Crisis & Emergency Resources

Rise Somatic Therapy is not an emergency or crisis service, and messages sent by email or through the client portal are not monitored continuously. If you are in crisis or experiencing a medical or mental health emergency, please use one of the resources below.

  • Emergency: Call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department for immediate medical or mental health emergencies.
  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: Call or text 988 for 24/7 crisis support, including emotional distress, suicidal thoughts, or mental health concerns.
  • Domestic Violence: National Domestic Violence Hotline — Call 800-799-SAFE (7233) or text START to 88788 for confidential support and safety planning.
  • Eating Disorders: National Alliance for Eating Disorders — Call 866-662-1235 for support, resources, and treatment referrals.
  • Mental Health & Substance Use: SAMHSA National Helpline — Call 800-662-HELP (4357) for treatment information and referrals.
  • Grief & Loss: For support during overwhelming grief or loss, call or text 988 or connect with a local grief support group or counselor.
