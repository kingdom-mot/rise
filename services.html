How I Can Help

Services & Approach

Each person's path looks different. I draw from the modalities below to meet you where you are and support the work that feels most meaningful to you.

  • INDIVIDUAL THERAPY

    Support for adolescents and adults

    Navigating trauma, relationships, body image, identity, and life transitions.

  • SOMATIC & EMBODIED THERAPY

    The connection between body and mind

    Exploring the connection between your body, emotions, thoughts, and experiences.

  • EMDR

    Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing

    Supporting the processing and integration of distressing traumatic experiences.

  • TRAUMA-CONSCIOUS YOGA

    Gentle, choice-based movement

    Body awareness incorporated when clinically appropriate.

  • ADDITIONAL APPROACHES

    ACT · DBT · Trauma-Informed Therapy

    Drawn on as needed to support your specific goals and history.
