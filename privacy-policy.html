Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Rise Somatic Therapy ("Rise," "we," "us") handles information collected through this website, risesomatictherapy.com (the "Site"). It does not describe how protected health information is handled within the course of clinical treatment — that is governed separately by the Notice of Privacy Practices provided to clients at the start of care, in accordance with HIPAA.

Information we collect

  • Information you provide directly: your name, email address, phone number, and any message content if you contact us by email or phone.
  • Automatically collected information: basic technical data such as browser type, device type, and pages visited, which may be collected through standard website analytics tools.
  • Scheduling information: if you use the "Schedule an appointment" link, you will be taken to a third-party client portal (clientsecure.me), which has its own privacy policy governing the information you submit there.

How we use information

We use the information collected through this Site only to respond to inquiries, communicate about scheduling, and understand general Site usage to improve the Site. We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

Appointment scheduling and client intake are handled through a third-party platform at marie-clifton.clientsecure.me. Your use of that platform is subject to its own privacy policy and terms, which we encourage you to review.

Cookies & analytics

This Site may use cookies or similar technologies for basic functionality and analytics. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect information submitted through this Site, but no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. Please avoid sending sensitive health details by email; use the secure client portal for anything related to your care.

Children's privacy

This Site is not directed at children under 13. Individual therapy services for adolescents are provided only within the clinical relationship, with parent or guardian involvement as required by law and clinical policy.

Your choices

You may contact us at any time to ask what information we hold about you from Site inquiries, or to request that it be deleted, subject to any recordkeeping obligations that apply to clinical records.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to risesomatics@gmail.com or 816-368-1733.

This page is a general template and is not a substitute for legal advice. We recommend having this policy, along with your clinical Notice of Privacy Practices, reviewed by an attorney familiar with HIPAA and your state's telehealth and social work licensing requirements before publishing.
