Privacy & Confidentiality

Last updated: September 2026

Your privacy matters. Rise Somatic Therapy is committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of your information.

Please note that information submitted through this website, including contact forms or appointment requests, may not be secure or confidential. Please avoid sharing sensitive or clinical information through website forms, email, or text. These methods should not be used for emergencies or urgent mental health concerns.

Information shared during therapy is protected by applicable federal and state confidentiality laws, including HIPAA, subject to legally required exceptions.

By contacting Rise Somatic Therapy through this website, you acknowledge that electronic communication carries some inherent privacy and security risks.

This page is a general overview and is not a substitute for legal advice. We recommend having this page, along with your clinical Notice of Privacy Practices, reviewed by an attorney familiar with HIPAA and your state's telehealth and social work licensing requirements.
